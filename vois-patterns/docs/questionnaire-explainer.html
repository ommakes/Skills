Questionnaire Picker

Someone else wrote the questions. The respondent answers them one per screen. Describe a question, and this page picks the component and shows how it behaves.

1. Pick a component

This runs the same code as question-engine.mjs. The first rule that matches wins. Rule numbers are PATH-H-2.

2. Likert: 5 points sit side by side, 7 stack

Every point has a word label. A 7 point scale is always vertical, because seven labelled points cannot fit across a phone and stay at 44px or more.

3. Rank: press and hold to drag, or use the arrows

Every item is in the list from the start. Hold a row for 200ms, then drag. Rows shift as you move. The arrows are the no-drag way. The answer stays empty until you move something or keep the order. Up to 6 items, rank all. From 7 to 12, the top 3 count. Above 12, do not rank.

4. Multi-select: the limit disables, it never drops a pick

Say the rule before the answer. Show a live count. At the maximum, disable the rest and say why.

5. Poll and quiz results

Results show only after the respondent answers. Rows keep their order. The correct answer has a check and the words, not just a color. Under 10 votes, no numbers show.

6. The question as data

This is the object the schema checks. It is built from your choices in step 1.
