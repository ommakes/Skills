## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added

- **Change Blindness** (`change-blindness`) in the cognitive and behavioral principles in `data/principles.json`. People often miss a change that happens during an interruption, such as an instant swap of the whole view, so a visible link between two states (a travelling element, a label that keeps its shared letters) lets the eye follow what changed. It also backs the remove-the-motion review in `vois-patterns/references/motion.md`: take the motion out and ask whether the user still knows what changed.
