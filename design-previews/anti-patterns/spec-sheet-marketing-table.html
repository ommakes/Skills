Anti-pattern · DS-SLOP-007

Spec-sheet tables for marketing

A multi-row table with a label column and a value column, used to present features or plans on a marketing surface, is boilerplate. Use grouped 2-column cards, scroll-snap pills, or clustered stats instead. (This doesn't apply to genuine data tables in product UI — those are governed by vois-patterns table/list patterns.)
