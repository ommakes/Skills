Anti-pattern · DS-SLOP-004

Eyebrow overuse

The uppercase, letter-spaced "eyebrow" label above a heading is fine once. It becomes a tell when it sits above every section. Cap it at roughly one per three sections — if every section has one, they've stopped meaning anything.
