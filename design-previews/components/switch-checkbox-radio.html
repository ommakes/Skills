Components · Job 10 · Binary preference

Switch vs Checkbox vs Radio

Switch means "this changes right now" — no submit step. Checkbox means "this changes when I submit the form" or records consent. Radio means the options are mutually exclusive.

Switch · takes effect immediately

Toggling this fires the change now — no Save button involved.

Checkbox · submitted with the form

Nothing happens until the surrounding form is submitted.

Radio · mutually exclusive

Choosing one always deselects the other — can't hold both at once.
