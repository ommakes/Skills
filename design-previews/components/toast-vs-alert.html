Toast vs Alert

Toast disappears before the user can necessarily act on it — use it only when there's no action to take. Alert stays on screen until dismissed, which is what an action requires.

Toast · no action required

Confirms something happened. Auto-dismisses in 3s — fine, because there's nothing to do about it.

Alert · action required, persistent

Your card was declined

Update your payment method to keep this subscription active.

Stays until the user acts or explicitly dismisses it — never auto-clears.
