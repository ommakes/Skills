Badge vs Dismissible Badge (Chip)

A read-only Badge is a label, not an action — it should never look clickable. A dismissible badge (Chip) carries real interaction weight and needs its own affordance; don't reach for a plain Button to represent a removable filter, it carries the wrong visual weight.
