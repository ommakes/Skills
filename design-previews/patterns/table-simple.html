Table, simple

Six columns or fewer, mostly read. The page scrolls and the header sticks to the page. No overflow on the wrapper, so there is no sideways scroll. Search, one filter, sort, 25 per page. Scroll the page to see the header stay put.

The wrapper deliberately sets no overflow. Adding overflow-x: auto would make it the scroll owner and the header would stop sticking to the page (DS-TABLE-001, DS-TABLE-004).
